The publication
Why the readers wanted a table.

Every week The Device Files takes on the questions nobody else is writing plainly about: when a wearable becomes a regulated medical device, and who decides; whether wearable makers should set their own signal standards before regulators do; who is accountable when AI out-diagnoses the doctor; what it takes to commercialize a device and get it reimbursed; what happens when Apple, Samsung and Oura move deeper into regulated health.

The tables
Not the usual executive dinner.

No one at the table is there to be sold to, and no one sits through a pitch. That is why the people you want actually come: a real conversation among peers, hosted by Blythe Karow, at a place worth the evening on its own.

Each table is 15 to 20 people and every seat is chosen. Invitation only. The evening carries past the night, into the essay, the podcast and the conversation that follows.

Experience · 01 · MedTech World

Malta

Friday, November 13, 2026 · Lunch · Terrone, Birgu
The gathering

MedTech Malta draws an international crowd, over 2,000 people from Europe, North America and the Middle East: founders, investors and clinicians alike. This table seats founders identified by The Device Files with the investors and strategics who should know them.

The lunch

Across the Grand Harbour from Valletta sit three neighborhoods known for their charm, their yachts and their history. We cross by water taxi to Birgu, wander the harbor, and settle in at Terrone, a local favorite for fresh seafood with a mention in the Michelin Guide, over a long lunch on the water.

Experience · 02 · CES

Las Vegas

January 2027 · Dinner · Co-hosted with Maverick Health Policy
The gathering

CES is the largest consumer technology stage in the world, and every year more of its companies edge toward regulated MedTech: wearables and devices that want to make a medical claim. This dinner seats the ones making that move with the people who know what it takes, in regulatory, quality, cybersecurity and health policy.

The dinner

Away from the roar of the show floor, a private room at a restaurant near the Venetian, quiet enough to talk. We gather once the day on the floor is done.
